Our Story

Made for the few pieces worth keeping.

Tote & Luxe started from a simple frustration: too many "luxury" bags and jewelry built for a photo, not a life. We work with a small circle of leatherworkers and jewelers who care about the underside of a seam and the finish on a clasp just as much as the front of a piece.

From canvas totes for campus to stainless-steel and zirconia jewelry for a night out, every piece is chosen to hold up to daily wear — not just a photograph. That's the only kind of luxury we're interested in.

— The Tote & Luxe Studio
